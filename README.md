# Computer Vision for Food Adulteration Detection

Bachelor research project | Four unpublished research manuscripts

## Overview

This project investigates image-based methods for identifying signs of food adulteration. The four manuscripts explore deep vision transformers (DVT) in comparison with several image-classification and anomaly-detection approaches. They are university project work, not journal or conference publications. Their small initial comparisons do not establish real-world food safety performance.

## Comparisons covered

1. **DVT and CNNs**: compares transformer-based image analysis with convolutional neural networks for identifying adulteration patterns.
2. **DVT and transfer learning**: compares DVT with ResNet- and MobileNet-based transfer-learning approaches.
3. **DVT and GAN-based augmentation**: studies transformer classification against a workflow using generated images to augment training data alongside a CNN-based classifier.
4. **DVT and autoencoders**: explores detection of previously unseen adulteration patterns by comparing image classification with an anomaly-detection approach.
